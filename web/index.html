    From GIF to Palm.

    01 · Make it yours

    Add and preview.

    The preview shows the actual 160 × 160 pixels the Palm will display. Native black-and-white GIFs keep their existing pixels with Automatic selected.

    02 · Send it over

    Sync, then HotSync.

    Choose your connection and click Sync. Wait for the prompt to press the Palm’s physical HotSync button. Your GIFs stay on this computer.

    03 · Enjoy the motion

    Open Ade’s App.

    Pick an animation on the Palm. Tap the image to pause or play, use a scroll button to return to the list, and select Invert to swap black and white.

    Each sync sends your full collection as a new app. A device backup is saved first; every installed resource is read back and checked. Invert preview affects only this screen—the Palm has its own Invert checkbox.
